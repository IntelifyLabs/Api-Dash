# MEC / ShowHouse sequences, multi threaded (17 Sept 2026)

Three variants. Each variant has **two threads**, because 84 companies have two
reachable decision makers. Every thread is a first email plus two follow ups.
The two people at one company never receive the same words, the same subject or
the same angle, and never on the same day.

# The CTA ladder

Every close asks for something small that returns something concrete. None of them
asks for a meeting, and none of them is a bump.

| Email | Ask | Why it converts |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shall I load your tiles into a version for you? | A yes costs them one word and gets them an asset |
| 2 | Reply with one collection name and I will set it up | The smallest possible reply that starts real work |
| 3 | Either a name to forward to, or tell me I am wrong | Two easy answers, both of which are a reply |

The trial is the CTA throughout. The link is in every follow up and is always
framed as the generic version, with theirs described as the thing worth having.
That framing is what turns a click into a conversation, because the visitor can
see what is missing and the missing piece has their name on it.

# Rules held throughout

- **No dashes of any kind** in any body. Checked by script.
- **No filtered words.** Nothing here says free, offer, guarantee, exclusive,
  limited, instant, discount, act now, click here, risk or opportunity. The trial
  is described as a version with their tiles in it, never as a free trial.
- **First emails are under 90 words.** Short paragraphs, one idea each, plenty of
  white space. They read the same on a phone as on a laptop.
- **No signature block, no images, no attachments, no tracking links.** One plain
  URL in the follow ups, never in email 1.
- **Two people at one company never get the same sentence.** Thread A is the
  customer journey and the lost visitor. Thread B is the collection, the craft and
  the internal cost. Different subject, different angle, different days.
- **Honest proof only.** The only figures used anywhere are the two published for
  MEC, seven weeks and three people. Daltile, Marazzi and Roomvo are verified.
- **AI is never the opening word.** Every competitor leads with it.
