# MEC_Instantly_Wave2.csv

**390 rows, one per contact.** 313 companies, 84 of which carry two decision
makers. Generated by `build_instantly.py` from `../q1/contacts_final.csv`.

## Contact mapping, corrected before building

| Column in the Clay export | Actually belongs to | Match rate |
|---|---|---|
| `Work Email (2)` | **person 1** | 287 of 299 |
| `Work Email` | **person 2** | 95 of 100 |

The two columns are crossed. Earlier in this campaign they were read as two
providers for one person, which was wrong: they are two different people. A
merge on the obvious pairing puts every greeting on the wrong address.

## Columns

**Send fields:** `email`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `company_name`, `website`

**Routing:** `contact_thread` (A or B), `segment_variant` (A, B or C),
`send_day_1/2/3` (1, 6, 13 for thread A; 3, 9, 16 for thread B)

**Copy:** `msg_subject_1/2/3`, `msg_body_1/2/3`. The `msg_` prefix is
deliberate. `email_subject` and `email_body` collide with Instantly built-ins,
and no header contains a space.

**QA, not for sending:** `qa_tool_level`, `qa_has_tryon`, `qa_tool_name`,
`qa_hook_quality`, `qa_hook`, `qa_partner_email`, `qa_country`, `qa_size`,
`qa_evidence`

## How to check it fast

Sort on `qa_hook_quality`.

**188 rows read `strong`.** The first line of email 1 is that company's own
sentence, quoted from `qa_evidence`, or the real name of their own tool. Spot
check ten and the rest will hold.

**202 rows read `weak`.** No usable quote came out of the evidence, so the row
fell back to a line that is still true but generic. These send safely and they
are the ones worth an hour of hand editing. `qa_evidence` is in the row, so the
better opening is usually sitting right there.

Then sort on `qa_partner_email`. Non-blank means a second person at the same
company is also in the file. Confirm the two rows carry different subjects and
different bodies before you upload, because they will get forwarded.

## Segments

| | Rows | Who | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| C | 80 | Already run a visualiser | Never suggest they lack a tool. Praise it, sell the missing half |
| A | 37 | A person does the visualising today | Replace the manual step, not the craft |
| B | 273 | No design step, or filters only | Open on what they promise, not what they lack |

`has_tryon` outranks `tool_level` on purpose. A company can read ADVANCED and
still be missing the generative half, and that cohort needs the opposite
message from one with no tool at all.

## Checks that passed on all 390 rows

- Zero dashes of any kind in any subject or body
- Zero filtered words, checked against the prospect's own quoted words too
- Every email 1 is 90 words or fewer, average 82
- `tryshowhouse.com` in every follow up, in no email 1
- Zero unfilled slots, zero duplicate addresses, zero blank first names

## Before you press send

1. **Open tryshowhouse.com yourself.** It is linked from 780 follow ups and I
   could not load it from here. Confirm it shows the written-description half
   and not only photo upload, that nothing behind it asks for payment details,
   and that it opens on a phone.
2. **Turn click tracking off.** A tracking redirect on a low reputation TLD is
   a worse signal than the plain link already in the copy.
3. **Check inbox placement first**, per the open audit flag on campaign 2.
   Deploying good copy into a spam foldering domain hides the real cause for
   another cycle.
4. **Job titles are still missing.** Thread A goes to person 1 and thread B to
   person 2, which is arbitrary. With titles, thread A should go to marketing,
   ecommerce or export and thread B to design, product or the owner.
