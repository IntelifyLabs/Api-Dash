## The angle, corrected

Showhouse is a white-label AI visualiser **and lead engine**. A shopper
photographs their own room, the brand's product appears in it, a guided finder
turns their answers into a written brief, and the image unlocks only once they
verify an email. The brand receives render, brief and a real address as one
record.

The two earlier framings were each half right and wrong on their own. The first
sold "a design tool", which is a feature Daltile and Marazzi give away free.
The second sold the lead record, which is true but abstract in line one. The
site resolves it in a sentence: **a visualiser is the hook, the pipeline is the
product.**

So the copy **opens on the mechanic**, because your tile on their own floor is
instantly picturable, and **closes on the pipeline**, because that is what pays
for it.

## What the page fixed that no rewrite would have

1. **The product is called Showhouse.** Nothing in v1 or v2 said so. Now named
   in message 1 on all 390 rows.
2. **`/stone` and `/bathroom` do not exist.** Both were guesses and both were
   404s. Real studios: `/tile`, `/rugs`, `/wallpaper`, `/countertops`,
   `/mosaic`. Stone routes to `/tile`, which is where the site puts it: "tile
   showrooms, stone yards, surface distributors".
3. **There is no self-serve trial**, so "start a trial" was an offer we could
   not honour. The real ladder is: open a live studio, which needs no sign in,
   then a demo where the team arrives with the prospect's catalogue loaded.
4. **The studios render a visitor's own photo.** That makes message 1's ask
   genuinely free: open a page, upload a photo.

Wording is borrowed from the page where it is sharper than mine: *before they
order a sample*, *every wall becomes a live product page*, *without touching
anything else in the photo*, *live in weeks, not quarters*.

## Message 1 is an A/B, both arms in this one file

Every row carries **two complete message 1s**. Instantly picks one per
contact, so the test runs off a single upload with no second campaign and no
split list.

| Arm | Columns | What it argues |
|---|---|---|
| **R, revenue** | `msg_subject_1a`, `msg_body_1a` | The visitor you never meet. They look, they cannot picture it in their own room, they leave with no name attached. |
| **C, cost** | `msg_subject_1b`, `msg_body_1b` | The sample you post to someone who was never going to order. Put the decision in front of the sample. |

`ab_arm` records which arm the row was dealt. The split is **by company**, so
both people at a two-person company are always on the same arm and never get
two different pitches: 154 revenue, 152 cost, zero companies split. It is
dealt **within each segment** rather than by a coin flip, because a flip left
segment C at 33 against 41 and the result would not have been readable.

Subject and body are paired, not mixed. A cost subject over a revenue body
would move two variables at once and neither number would mean anything.

- **`msg_subject_1a` (revenue)** leads on what the catalogue cannot do:
  *A PDF, or their actual bathroom* · *They can't picture it. That is the
  problem* · *Stop asking them to imagine it* · *Your collections, in their own
  room* · *Your catalogue can't show their room*
- **`msg_subject_1b` (cost)** wraps the same product in the show each row came
  from: *Before Cersaie 2027, let them see it* · *Your products in their rooms
  by Cersaie 2027* · *Stop posting samples before Cersaie 2027*, and for the 20
  rows with no sourced show, *Before the next sample goes out* · *Let them see
  it before you post it* · *The samples that never become orders*.

The show label comes from the row's own source directory. Cersaie 2026 ran 21
to 25 September 2026, so the forward reference is the 2027 edition: for a
manufacturer who just came off a stand that is the planning window, not a
deadline a year out.

Follow-ups are single copies, `msg_subject_2/3` and `msg_body_2/3`. Only
message 1 is under test.

## Checks passing on all 390 rows

Zero dashes of any kind. Zero filtered words, checked against the prospect's
own quoted words too. Message 1 runs **155 to 180 words**, the ceiling before a
cold reader starts skimming. Every message 1 subject is 50 characters or fewer
so phones do not truncate it, and none starts lower case. A studio link in
every message, the product named in every message 1, and zero unfilled merge
slots, duplicate addresses or shouted greetings.

At the 65 two-person companies, **zero pairs share a subject or a body** on any
of the four messages. The two arms are never identical on the same row. No
single message 1 subject takes more than a quarter of the file: 24 per cent on
the revenue arm, 15 on the cost arm.

**The build is reproducible.** Running `build_instantly.py` twice produces a
byte-identical CSV, so a rebuild never moves a contact from one arm to the
other mid-test.

## Bugs caught building v3

- **The MEC claim was attached to the wrong studio.** Message 3 said "The Tile
  Studio is the original build, running for MEC Artworks today", because it
  interpolated whichever studio the row routed to. The Mosaic Studio is the MEC
  build. Now hard-coded, and asserted at build time.
- **Double punctuation in the quote**: `"Solicita una reunión con nosotros.".`
  Trailing stops are stripped before quoting.
- **220 of 390 rows shared one message-1 subject**, because variant B is 273
  rows and had a single fixed string. That is campaign 2's copy defect four
  returning. Message 1 now draws from a bank as well.

## Bugs caught building v4

- **`hash()` randomises per process.** Python salts string hashing unless
  `PYTHONHASHSEED` is set, so the A/B arms and every rotated subject reshuffled
  on each rebuild. A contact on the revenue arm today could be on the cost arm
  tomorrow with nothing in the file showing it moved, which invalidates a
  running test in silence. Replaced with CRC32. Two builds are now identical.
- **Both people at a two-person company got the same message 1**, introduced by
  the arm rewrite. Thread B now opens on the outcome and closes by naming the
  colleague, so the two read as two people writing rather than one merge.
- **The 20 rows with no trade show had one cost subject between them**, because
  every show-bearing line dropped out and a single survivor was left. Four
  dateless lines added to that bank.
- **`Work Email` and `Work Email (2)` are two different people, and the columns
  are crossed.** They were read as two addresses for one person.
- **A second brand after a separator read as a merge field.** "GTILES -
  ROCKYLAND" and "Roca / Lamosa USA" wrote out in full mid-sentence, and the
  spaced hyphen was the only dash left in the file. Both now use the first
  segment, which is the brand the domain belongs to.
- **The `niche` column poisoned studio routing for the second time.** Every
  Cersaie row carries "Ceramic / Tile (verify)", which sent an interior-doors
  manufacturer to the tile studio. Dropped from routing entirely.
