## The one thing the feedback asked for that is not in here

**A result from MEC.** The case study publishes no performance figures, only
seven weeks and a team of three, so that is all message three claims. Per the
honest-proof rule nothing else was invented.

**If Abdullah can get one real number out of MEC** (leads captured per month,
or time on page before and after) message three in both threads becomes
materially stronger, and it is the single highest-value thing missing from this
copy. Everything else is written.

## Blocking check before send

**The five studio paths are proposed, not verified.** `tryshowhouse.com` cannot
be loaded from this environment, the proxy answers 403 to CONNECT, so
`/tile`, `/mosaic`, `/stone`, `/wallpaper` and `/bathroom` are guesses at the
URL structure. Correct them in the `STUDIO` dict at the top of
`build_instantly.py` and rebuild. **A wrong path sends 390 people to a 404,
which is worse than sending them nowhere.**

While you are on the site, the value props in this copy came from the feedback
rather than from the page, so confirm the wording matches what the product
actually does: the lead record, the dashboard, and the catalogue load.

## How to review it

Sort on `qa_hook_quality`. **188 strong**: message 1 opens on that company's own
sentence or the real name of their own tool. **202 weak**: no usable quote came
out of the evidence, so the row fell back to a line that is still true but
generic. `qa_evidence` sits in the row, so the better opening is usually there.

Then sort on `qa_studio`. **29 blank rows** link to the homepage because they
make something with no studio page, doors and sanitaryware mostly. Decide
whether to send those at all.

Then sort on `qa_partner_email`. Non-blank means the second person at that
company is also in the file, on a different argument and a different day.

## Checks that passed on all 390 rows

- Zero dashes of any kind, zero filtered words, checked against the prospect's
  own quoted words too
- Every message 1 is 100 words or fewer, average 89
- The link is in all three messages on every row
- Zero unfilled slots, zero duplicate addresses, zero lower-case or shouted
  greetings

## Bugs caught while building v2

1. **`niche` poisoned the studio routing.** It is a source label, not evidence:
   every Cersaie row carries "Ceramic / Tile (verify)", which sent AIP Porte, a
   maker of interior doors, to the tile studio. Second time that column has
   broken a classifier in this campaign. It is no longer consulted.
2. **"wall coverings" is not a wallpaper signal.** Every tile company writes it,
   so Aparici, a ceramic tile maker, was routed to `/wallpaper`. Wallpaper now
   needs the actual word.
3. **"Hello juan,"** — the waterfall returns names in the source's own case.
   Folded per token, so "Maher K." and "alessandro albasini" both come out
   right while O'Brien and de Vries are left alone.
4. **Follow-up subject lines collapsed** to 4 and 2 distinct strings across 390
   rows, which is copy defect number four from campaign 2 creeping back in.
   Each follow-up now draws from a bank keyed on a hash of the address, so the
   choice is stable across rebuilds and the two people at one company still
   land on different lines.
