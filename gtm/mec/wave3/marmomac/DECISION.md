# Marmomac: dropped, 6 Oct 2026

**Verdict: not a source for this campaign.** Stopped after six halls.

## What was checked

1,096 exhibitors on the Brandrooms portal. Six halls pasted and read in full,
**562 companies, 51% of the fair. Zero prospects.**

| Hall | Companies | What is actually in it |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | 197 | China pavilion. Diamond tools, machinery, 40-odd Imp & Exp trading houses |
| 7 | 133 | Diamond tools, abrasives, resins, chemicals, sealants |
| 9 | 121 | Quarries, block traders, marble and granite exporters |
| 2 | 47 | Machinery, metalwork, waterjet, robotics, electronics |
| 5 | 36 | Sawing, polishing and handling machinery, CAD, surveying |
| 1 | 28 | Brazil pavilion. Granite and marble block and slab exporters |

Three rows were worth keeping by name, all engineered or branded surface
houses in hall 9: **Cimstone**, **Pokarna** (Quantra) and **Lundhs**. Three
companies does not justify mining a fair.

## Why it fails

**Marmomac is a supply chain fair, not a brand fair.** Its exhibitors sell to
each other: quarries sell blocks to processors, processors sell slabs to
fabricators, tool and machinery builders sell to both. Almost none of them
runs a consumer-facing website with a catalogue a shopper browses, which is
the one thing a visualiser needs to sit on. The pitch has nowhere to land.

That is a property of the fair, not of the stone vertical. The branded surface
houses that would qualify — Antolini, Margraf, Laminam, Cosentino, Neolith,
Caesarstone — were never found in the six halls read.

## Left unchecked

534 companies across halls 6, 10, 11, 12, 4, 3, Area B, Area D, Avenue E,
Area A and five small ones. No verdict was given to any of them, because the
lesson from hall 1 was that hall contents cannot be predicted.

**To revisit cheaply:** type `Antolini`, `Margraf`, `Laminam` or `Cosentino`
into the portal's own company search. If several exhibit, the unseen halls are
worth pasting and this decision should be reopened. If they do not, it is
closed for good.

## The reusable test

Before mining any directory, ask whether its exhibitors are **brands or
suppliers**. A fair whose attendees buy from each other produces no rows for a
campaign that needs companies selling to end consumers. Checking two or three
halls first costs nothing and would have saved this one.

Applied to the remaining plan:

- **Heimtextil** wallcoverings and carpets. Brands. A.S. Création alone is
  2,000 designs a year and already runs a room visualiser, which is the
  has-a-tool hook rather than a rejection. Proven profile, source in hand.
- **Salone del Mobile.** Brands by definition; its exhibitors are consumer
  furniture houses.
- **spoga+gafa** garden living and garden unique. Brands. The creation & care
  segment is suppliers and was already excluded.
